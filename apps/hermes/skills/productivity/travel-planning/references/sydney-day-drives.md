## Less-obvious candidates (suggested, not yet confirmed as done)
- **Central Coast hinterland loop:** Old Northern Rd, Wisemans Ferry Rd, Mangrove Mountain, Kulnura, George Downes Drive, then the Yarramalong Valley to Wyong, returning on the M1. It's a popular motorcycle route.
  - Kulnura One-Stop Jerry's Café is open 6:30am–3pm.
  - Bambino's Cafe is at 993 George Downes Dr, Kulnura.
- **Megalong Valley:** via Blackheath, about 1.5 hr. Megalong Road descends through tight switchbacks.
  - Megalong Valley Tea Rooms are at 824 Megalong Rd and sell LOT 101 farm produce.
- **Putty Road:** from Windsor through Wollemi National Park. The Grey Gum International Café at 8679 Putty Rd is about 2 hr away; a post listed it as open 7am–2pm daily. For roughly 1 hr each way, turn back at Colo Heights. There's no mobile coverage and few chargers.
