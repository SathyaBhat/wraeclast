# Fiji Marriott-family comparison — Christmas 2026 example

**Purpose:** reusable example of a relaxation-first comparison using live, date-specific pricing.

## Search assumptions

- Two adults, one room
- Five nights: 21–26 December 2026
- Origin: Sydney
- Target room budget: A$500–600/night
- Snapshot retrieved: 24 August 2026
- Conversion reference observed: US$1 = A$1.41636; displayed Fiji tax: 12.5%

## Findings

| Property / room | Rate shown | Indicative total after tax and FX | Planning conclusion |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Sheraton Resort & Spa, Tokoriki Island — Ocean View King, breakfast | US$374/night | A$596/night; A$2,980/5 nights | Best fit: island setting and adults-only wing; ferry transfer extra |
| Tokoriki — Island View King, breakfast | US$333/night | A$531/night; A$2,653/5 nights | Budget fallback |
| Sheraton Fiji Golf & Beach Resort, Denarau — Garden King, room-only | US$353/night | A$562/night; A$2,812/5 nights | Convenient but less secluded; breakfast pushed it above target |
| Fiji Marriott Resort Momi Bay — Lagoon Front King, room-only | US$427/night | A$680/night; A$3,402/5 nights | Over target at Christmas |

## Operational observations

- Fiji Airways lists a direct SYD–NAN time of about four hours; quoted promotional fares were non-peak, so they were not represented as Christmas pricing.
- Tokoriki is reached by a roughly one-hour ferry from Port Denarau. This is the key convenience trade-off.
- The checked Tokoriki rate showed flexible cancellation; the lower Sheraton Fiji rates shown were non-refundable.
- Hotel brand pages were suitable for facilities/access claims but did not render reliably in automated scrapers. Date-specific Booking.com pages contained usable live room, tax, inclusion and cancellation information.
